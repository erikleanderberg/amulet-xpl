Why the LRA is silent

Two independent faults stacked on top of each other, and a stale bridge hid both. Neither one is a broken board.

Current state: the XIAO enumerates and runs, but no new firmware has reached it. The XIAO-SENSE bootloader drive has not mounted once, so every flash attempt had nothing to write to.

Fault one

The flashed sketch drives the wrong three pins

The last thing uploaded was ~/Downloads/max98357a_xiao_nrf52840, a standalone amp test. It routes I²S to D1 / D2 / D3. Your amp is soldered to D8 / D9 / D10. Even a perfect upload would have left the LRA dead still — the clocks come out on pins with nothing attached.

Fault two

Nothing is reaching the chip

Every flash route needs the board in its UF2 bootloader. It never got there. arduino-cli upload races the re-enumeration, and the 1200-baud touch resets a crashed app straight back into itself rather than into DFU — adafruit-nrfutil answered with "Target is not in DFU mode."

What hid it: a bench bridge left running since 21 Sep kept reporting #status,connected — a two-day-old claim about a port that had re-enumerated underneath it. A silent board looked like a connected one.

The way out

Forcing the bootloader

  1. Disconnect the LiPo. RESET does not cut power while a cell is attached, so a wedged app never gets a true power cycle — and an unfused cell on the BAT pads is the same hazard that took the last two boards.
  2. Leave USB-C plugged in. That is what the drive appears on.
  3. Double-tap RESET — two firm presses about 300 ms apart. Roughly a double-click rhythm. Too slow reads as two resets, too fast as one. Three or four attempts is normal.
  4. Watch for a drive called XIAO-SENSE. That is the whole signal that it worked.

Once it flashes

Driving the LRA

bench3_live streams telemetry immediately and advertises as Amulet-XPL, so you will know within a second that it took. Commands are single characters over USB serial or the bridge's /cmd.

KeyDoes
bSingle tap, 150 ms. No cooldown. Start here.
tTone at 160 Hz for 3 s
zBuzz toggle — 250 ms on / 250 ms off, 5 min cap
oContinuous hold, 60 s cap (coil heat)
w / nSweep 40–300 Hz / 120–210 Hz
sStop everything
+ / −Volume in 0.1 steps
hStatus line

Coil envelope: 160 Hz is the Drake's resonance, so it moves hardest there and will feel far stronger than the amp sketch's 440 Hz default. Firmware starts at volume 0.60 and enforces COIL_MAX_VPP 7.0. With Vin on the battery, SUPPLY_VOLTS 4.2 is correct and the 0.83 ceiling is the right one — no rebuild needed.
