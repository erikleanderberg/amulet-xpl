Amulet Pulse Wiring

Four jumper wires from the DFRobot heart-rate and oximeter sensor straight to the XIAO. No driver board, no resistors, nothing in between.

Sensor DFRobot SEN0518 (Gravity, MAX30102 chip) Talks over I²C, address 0x57 Power 3.3 V from the XIAO Labels checked against DFRobot wiki

What changed from the last page

  • The "MAX30102" box was your DFRobot sensor all along. MAX30102 is the chip on it. It's now labelled by the product you have.
  • The purple "Pulse Sensor" on D1 is gone. That was the Adafruit analog backup. You're not using it, so D1 is free.
  • Data wire colours fixed. The last page had green and blue swapped. The Gravity cable is green = D (data) and blue = C (clock). If you wired by colour from the old page, swap the two data wires.

Wire by the letters printed next to the connector, not by colour alone. Colours are what DFRobot's Gravity cable uses; loose jumpers can be any colour.

Do it

  1. Unplug USB from the XIAO.
  2. If the sensor board has a small I²C / UART switch, slide it to I²C. The same two pins become TX/RX in UART mode, which is why they're labelled D/T and C/R.
  3. Green: sensor D/T → XIAO D4.
  4. Blue: sensor C/R → XIAO D5.
  5. Black: sensor GND → − rail.
  6. Red: sensor VCC → + rail. That's 3.3 V. The sensor also takes 5 V, but don't use the XIAO's 5V pin: it would push 5 V onto the data lines, and the XIAO's pins aren't 5 V tolerant.
  7. Plug USB back in.

Easiest mistake: D and C swapped. Nothing breaks, the sensor just doesn't answer. If the test below finds nothing, swap green and blue at the XIAO end.

How you'll know it works

A test sketch scans I²C and should report a device at 0x57, then prints heart rate and SpO₂ with a fingertip resting still on the window. The readings take about 4 seconds to settle.

Open question from the project doc: DFRobot's library gives a computed heart rate every few seconds, not the beat-by-beat pulse waveform HRV needs. The same test tells us which one this board can give.
