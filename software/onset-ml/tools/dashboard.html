Haptic tests

Amp takes the signalSend any drive. Pass when the board reports a non-zero peak within 1.5 s. If it stays 0, the I²S pins or the firmware are wrong — not the wiring to the coil.—waiting
Tap is feltHold the LRA between two fingers, press Tap. A tap you cannot feel with the amp driving means amp power, SD, or the coil connection.your callnot run
Resonance tone is feltTone 160 Hz for 3 s. Should be clearly stronger than the tap. Back off Vol if it feels hard rather than louder.your callnot run
Where is it strongest?Run a sweep and press Mark at the step that feels strongest. Datasheet says near 160 Hz. Marks far from that mean the coil is loaded (glued, clamped) or it's a different actuator.—not run
Sensors stay quiet while the amp runsAutomatic. Sample-to-sample noise on the FSR and pulse channels during a drive vs. the second before it. Pass at ≤ 2× baseline. Fail = amp switching noise on the 3.3 V rail: give the amp its own supply or add a bulk cap.—waiting
Taps land on every heartbeatFinger on the pulse sensor, switch on Tap on every heartbeat. Pass after 10 beats when taps match beats within 10%.—off
